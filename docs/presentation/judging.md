## 8. Judging Process

Judging Process will happen in three stages:

Stage One: The first stage will determine via pass/fail whether the ideas meet a baseline level of viability, in that the Project reasonably fits the theme and reasonably used Meta Model API and/or Meta's open-weight models to build the project in the Hackathon.

Stage Two: All Submissions that pass Stage One will be evaluated in Stage Two based on the following criteria using a scale from 0-10:

**Judging Rubric**

### Impact (30%)

What's the real-world potential here? Who benefits, and how much does it matter? Could this actually become something people use? Does it fit into one of the problem statements listed above?

### Use of Meta AI Tooling (25%)

How creatively did this team use Meta's AI tooling? Did they go beyond a basic integration? Did they surface capabilities that surprised even us?

### Technical Depth & Execution (20%)

Is the engineering sound and thoughtfully refined? Does this feel like something that was thought through with real craft.

### Demo (25%)

Is this a working, impressive demo? Does the product hold up live? Is the demo video genuinely cool to watch?

Stage Three: The top 5 scoring projects per track move on to a final round of judging, where final round Judges will watch the video demos live and rank them using the same rubric.

### Bonus Prizes from Meta: $50K

An additional **$50K** will be reserved for bonus awards recognizing standout projects and community achievements.

### Most Viral Project — $20,000

- Rewards: the project that generates the highest engagement on X after the Event.
- Judged: post your project demo on X and submit the post link through the hackathon platform. The winner will be selected based on total engagement (likes, reposts, and comments) up until November 8 EOD.

### Best Build on Meta Open Weight Models — $10,000

- Rewards: the best project built using Muse Glimmer or Muse Spark Open, served on participants' own hardware or through partner-provided inference.
- Judged: projects are flagged during the first judging round. The infrastructure partner verifies that the project runs on an eligible Meta open-weight model.

### Community Vote — $10,000

- Rewards: the project that receives the most votes in the public gallery. The winner will be announced November 16.
- Judged: the winner is determined by the total number of votes received through the hackathon platform, limited to one vote per verified account.

### Best Agentic Use-Case — $5,000

- Rewards: the project that makes the strongest use of AI agents to complete meaningful tasks, make decisions, or coordinate multi-step workflows.
- Judged: judges evaluate the project's level of autonomy, effectiveness in completing its intended task, and practical usefulness.

### Best Creative Build — $5,000

- Rewards: the most original and compelling creative project built using Muse Image or Muse Wonder.
- Judged: projects are flagged during the first judging round and evaluated on creative originality, execution, and how effectively the model is used in the final experience.
