# Narration script — "One building, one day" (~259 words, cap 360)

Voice: Kokoro `am_adam`, speed 1.0. One WAV per chunk, aligned to shot
windows below. On-screen text never repeats a spoken sentence verbatim.
"0.0 K drift" in V07 is an illustrative demo numeral, not a measured result.

## V01 hook (0-5 s, S01) — 19 words

Every morning, buildings wake up hungry. Forty percent of America's carbon
comes from buildings like this one.

## V02 problem (5-20 s, S02) — 36 words

Heating and cooling eats forty to sixty percent of that — fifteen percent of
all energy on Earth. And most buildings still run like it's 1980, because a
controller tuned for one building fails everywhere else.

## V03 persona (20-30 s, S03) — 17 words

Meet the facility manager. One building, one headache — until one policy
learns to run them all.

## V04 equipment (scene 26-41 s, voice 30.4-40.6 s, S04: 2D s-control A/B) — 30 words

This is our building: three floors, thirty-nine zones, four air handlers.
Every room has a damper and a reheat valve. The policy sees them all — and
nothing else is hardcoded.

## V05 morning (45-70 s, S05) — 28 words

Six AM. The building wakes. Air handlers spin up, dampers sweep open, and
every zone glides into its comfort band before the first coffee is poured.

## V06 noon (70-95 s, S06) — 26 words

Noon. A heat wave hammers the glass. The policy pours cooling exactly where
bodies are, trimming each damper five-minute step by five-minute step.

## V07 payoff (95-105 s, S07) — 13 words

Then the cold snap hits. Reheat answers in seconds. Zero drift.
Zero complaints.

## V08 night (105-135 s, S08) — 29 words

Evening. People leave; the policy widens the eco band and lets the building
sleep — warm where it matters, dark where it doesn't. One day, one policy,
no tuning.

## V09 how-it-works (135-147 s, S09: 2D s-how diagram) — 24 words

How? One neural policy reads the floor plan and live sensors, then commands
every handler, valve, and boiler — same brain, any building.

## V09B built-with-meta (147-155 s, S09B: 2D s-meta) — 18 words

Meta AI did the building: rules and neural nets in PyTorch —
written with Muse, down to this video.

## V10 metrics (155-168 s, S10) — 22 words

The test: full thirty-day episodes across heat, cold, and swing weather.
Comfort, energy, carbon — every step scored.

## V11 claim (168-173 s, S11) — 4 words

One policy. Every building.

## V12 cta (173-178 s, S12) — 9 words
