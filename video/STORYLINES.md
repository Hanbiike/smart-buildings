# Storylines — smart-buildings 3-minute demo

Assumptions: 165-170 s, 16:9, Kokoro voice + MusicGen music (both free local
on RTX 5090, no paid items). All facts trace to docs/presentation/*. No stops
mode: building the recommendation (A); B and C kept for a later switch.

## A. One building, one day (RECOMMENDED — stranger-followable, codex-shaped)

- Device: a moment in someone's day fused with the-object-is-the-explanation.
  Form: a flight through one world that never cuts away from the building.
- The viewer rides one office building from dawn to night through a heat wave
  and a cold snap; each chapter dives inside to watch the policy act, and
  ends on comfortable people. Calm, inevitable, earned.
- First 2 s: pre-dawn aerial, one window lights up, numeral "40%" lands over
  the facade with the words "of US carbon is buildings".
- Beats: hook-numbers → problem-persona → x-ray reveal → morning ramp →
  noon heat → storm swing + people payoff (wow) → night eco → how-it-works →
  metrics → claim → end card.
- Look: architectural-model 3D under a real day-cycle sky; 2D beats on warm
  light ground with one thermal-amber accent. Sound: steady pulse score,
  airy whooshes on dives, soft chime when each comfort numeral lands.

## B. The result first

- Device: the result first. Form: an endless zoom that pulls back, then
  replays the day forward.
- Opens on a damper gliding shut and a comfort numeral ticking to zero
  deviation; the camera pulls back damper → zone → floor → building → city,
  then dives back in to replay the day that earned it. Confident, proof-led.
- First 2 s: macro of a VAV damper moving, "0.0 K drift" ticking beside it.
- Beats: result-macro → pullback-to-city → title → day replay chapters →
  people payoff → how-it-works → metrics → claim → end card.
- Look: same 3D building, macro-photography open; 2D ground darker slate.
  Sound: sub-bass pullback, bright resolve on the replay.

## C. Before and after, then one (bold)

- Device: life before and after. Form: split screen to one.
- Left half: the same building under old control, churning red, occupants
  fanning themselves; right half: our policy, calm blue. At midpoint the
  camera dives through the divider and the rest is one calm world.
- First 2 s: hard vertical split, same timestamp both sides, left sweating.
- Beats: split-hook → split chapters (morning/noon) → dive-through →
  single-world evening → people payoff → how-it-works → metrics → end card.
- Look: split grade (hot left, calm right) collapsing to one palette.
  Sound: grinding loop left, clean pulse right, single theme after the dive.
